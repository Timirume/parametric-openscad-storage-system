# Tolerance Study

## Objective
To ensure reliable lid fit across FDM printers.

## Selected Clearance
- 0.5mm radial clearance

## Justification
Based on typical FDM variance:
- Printer deviation: ±0.2mm
- Material expansion: ~0.1–0.3mm

Total safety margin: ~0.5mm

## Result
Balanced friction fit without excessive looseness.
